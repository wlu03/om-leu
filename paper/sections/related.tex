\section{Related work}

\paragraph{Latent constructs in choice models.} The idea that utility is composed over
quantities the analyst does not observe is the integrated choice and latent variable model
\citep{benakiva2002hybrid,walker2002grum}, and that literature has already settled what
counts as evidence for it. A latent construct is accepted when it is tied to the data by a
measurement equation with stated normalisations \citep{daly2012ordered}, when identification
is checked rather than assumed \citep{raveau2012identifiability}, and when the analyst
concedes that a better fit is not itself evidence of the construct
\citep{vij2016latently,chorus2014impossibility}. Our formulation in \Cref{sec:method} is an
instance of that family in which the measurement equation is a frozen language model, and we
adopt its standards rather than proposing new ones. Within the neural branch, taste
parameters have been made functions of covariates \citep{han2022tastenet}, a learned term has
been added to a specified utility \citep{sifringer2020enhancing}, and one boosted ensemble has
been fitted per alternative under a shared softmax \citep{salvade2024rumboost}.
\citet{hillel2021systematic} document how sensitive the resulting rankings are to the split,
which is the sensitivity our protocol addresses.

\paragraph{Concept bottlenecks and their leakage.} Routing a prediction through a named
intermediate layer is the concept bottleneck model \citep{koh2020concept}. That its soft
activations carry information beyond the named concepts is established
\citep{mahinpei2021promises}, as is the decomposition of the resulting accuracy gap into a
violated Markov assumption and an inflexible concept predictor, with an explicit side-channel
as the legitimate place for the remainder \citep{havasi2022addressing}. Post-hoc concept
bottleneck models make the same point empirically: the residual channel can supply nearly all
of the accuracy while the concept layer supplies the interpretation \citep{yuksekgonul2023post}.
A design in which an interpretable channel is added to an unconstrained one, with the second
up-weighted, is that side-channel under another name, and the number that has to be reported
is the interpretable channel's accuracy with the side-channel removed. Using a language model
to write the concept vocabulary is likewise established \citep{oikarinen2023labelfree,yang2023labo}.

\paragraph{Erasure.} Removing a linear subspace so that a downstream reader cannot recover it
runs from partialling out \citep{lovell1963seasonal} to iterative nullspace projection
\citep{ravfogel2020null} and the closed-form solution we use \citep{belrose2023leace}. Its
limits are known: guardedness under a linear probe does not imply guardedness under a
multinomial logit head \citep{ravfogel2023loglinear}, and nonlinear erasure remains unsolved
\citep{ravfogel2022kernelized}. We use erasure as an identification device and claim nothing
about the technique.

\paragraph{Combination and controls.} Pooling predictive distributions
\citep{wolpert1992stacked} with weights fitted out of fold \citep{vanderlaan2007super,
yao2018using} is standard, and \citet{geweke2011optimal} show a positive fitted weight is not
evidence that a component is informative. Controls that hold information fixed while removing
content have a long history outside this area: control tasks for probing
\citep{hewitt2019designing}, permutation tests within class \citep{ojala2010permutation}, and
random-feature baselines \citep{stoppiglia2003ranking}. Within text-feature work, hand-written
templates have been found to match language-model serialisations of the same fields
\citep{hegselmann2023tabllm} and random character strings to recover the gains attributed to
generated descriptors \citep{roth2023waffling}. The one control we could not find a precedent
for is the weight-matched uninformative channel of \Cref{sec:controls}.
