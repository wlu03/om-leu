\section{Identification by erasure}
\label{sec:identification}

The controls of \Cref{sec:controls} can show that a gain is not combination arithmetic and not
generic text. They cannot show that it is not a restatement of the attributes, because the
generator was shown the attributes and the text may encode them in a form the reader recovers.
We address that directly.

\paragraph{Construction.} Before the reader sees anything, each embedding is residualised on
the concept vector $c_{ij} = [\,1,\ \tilde x_{ij},\ z_i\,]$ with a separate least-squares map
per (slot $k$, canonical alternative $a$), fitted on the training rows:
\begin{equation}
e'_{ijk} \;=\; e_{ijk} - c_{ij}^{\top} W_{k,\,a(ij)},
\qquad
W = \arg\min_W \; \lVert E - cW \rVert^2 \ \text{on training rows}.
\label{eq:erase}
\end{equation}
Fitting per alternative also removes the per-alternative mean, so on the training rows the
residual is uncorrelated with the attributes, the alternative identity and the covariates, and
no linear reader recovers any of them. The shuffled control of an erased variant shuffles the
\emph{already erased} embeddings, so the control cannot reintroduce the attributes through the
subtracted term.

\paragraph{Verification.} A ridge probe on the concatenated slot embeddings recovers time,
cost, alternative identity and covariates with $R^2$ between $0.70$ and $0.99$ before erasure
and $0.000$ after, on both training and held-out rows; identity accuracy falls from $100\%$ to
chance. On \dataset{Amazon}, where the alternatives are catalogue products, price is recovered
at $R^2 = 0.50$ on held-out rows before erasure and $0.000$ after. That the generated text
encodes the price of the product it describes at half the held-out variance is itself the
quantitative form of the restatement concern.

\paragraph{What survives is the estimand.} After \Cref{eq:erase} the difference between the
text channel and its own shuffled control is a contribution that is, by construction, not
linearly recoverable from the alternative's attributes, its identity or the person covariates.
We report that difference as the identified contribution, and we report the difference between
the erased and unerased channels as the part of the raw gain that \emph{was} restatement.

\paragraph{A second device.} Independently of erasure, a member can be trained with
$\nu\,L_{ij}$ added to its utilities as a fixed offset, where $L$ is the cross-fitted attribute
log-probability. The member is then optimised only on what the attribute model does not
already predict. This targets redundancy rather than restatement, and we report it alongside.

\paragraph{Scope.} Erasure is linear. The claim is that the surviving contribution is not
linearly recoverable from the conditioning set; a nonlinear reader might still recover
something, and guardedness under a linear probe does not imply guardedness under a multinomial
logit head \citep{ravfogel2023loglinear}. We state this rather than overclaim, and we note the
stronger design that would remove the caveat: withhold a designated attribute subset from the
generator entirely, so that an exclusion restriction rather than a projection does the work. We
have not run that design.
